## Full description

You don't drive the bus — you run the company!

**Bus Tycoon: Transport Empire** is a transport management (tycoon) game. Found your company, pick its name and logo, and start in one of 10 big Turkish cities with an empty garage and ₺1.6 million. Buy your first minibus (dolmuş), open a route on the city's real streets, set the right fare and grow your fleet all the way to intercity coaches.

**START IN YOUR CITY**
• İstanbul, Ankara, İzmir, Bursa, Antalya, Adana, Konya, Gaziantep, Kayseri, Samsun
• Real street maps: tap two stops and the route follows the shortest way through the streets
• Stops along the way are added for you; district centres and busy neighbourhoods mean more riders
• A 5-step tutorial walks you through your first route

**FAMOUS LANDMARKS AND SPECIAL DAYS**
• 134 famous landmarks: palaces, mosques, stadiums, towers, bazaars, universities, malls
• Routes passing near landmarks carry more passengers
• Mosques draw crowds on holidays, stadiums on match days, tourist sights in summer
• Zoom in and landmarks appear as 3D models on the map

**FROM MINIBUS TO INTERCITY**
• 13 vehicles: minibus, midibus, city bus (including electric and articulated), intercity, comfort, VIP and double-decker
• Every vehicle has a role: minibuses pay off on short routes, city buses carry busy lines
• Buy new or used, trade in, overhaul or sell
• Manage breakdowns, wear, fuel, drivers and terminal fees

**THE HEART OF THE GAME: TICKET PRICE**
• Higher fares mean fewer passengers; cheap tickets fill the bus but leave money on the table
• A live profit curve with an "ideal zone" helps you find the sweet spot
• Fair prices and comfortable vehicles raise your reputation, and reputation raises demand everywhere

**FROM CITY TO COUNTRY TO EUROPE**
• License more cities and connect them with bus routes
• All 81 provinces of Türkiye plus 140 European cities: 221 cities in 36 countries
• Your own bus terminal in every city: 16 upgrades raise its popularity

**EASY TO MANAGE**
• The route planner shows length, riders, fare, occupancy and expected profit on one card
• One-glance route status: "Not enough capacity", "Low occupancy", "Losing money"
• The maintenance assistant overhauls worn vehicles and repairs broken ones, sending them back to their routes
• An 18-goal chain unlocks the game step by step

**CLEAN AND SMOOTH**
• A dark, clean interface designed for phones and tablets
• Vehicles driving along your routes, day-night cycle
• Currency: Turkish lira

**GOAL: ₺400,000,000**
Reach 400 million lira in cash and become the Bus Emperor! Careful: stay in the red for 30 days and you go bankrupt.

• Fully offline, no internet needed
• No ads; optional small shop: looks packs, city license, capital
• Automatic saving
• Interface language: Turkish
