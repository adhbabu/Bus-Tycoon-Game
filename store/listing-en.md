## Full description

You don't drive the bus — you run the company!

**Bus Tycoon: Transport Empire** is a transport management (tycoon) game on a real map of Türkiye and Europe. Start with a used minibus and a small budget, connect cities with routes, find the right ticket price and grow your fleet all the way to luxury VIP coaches.

**REAL MAP, REAL CITIES**
• All 81 provinces of Türkiye plus 140 cities in 35 European countries: 221 cities in 36 countries
• Population and distance drive passenger demand
• Level up, buy operating licenses and expand across borders

**THE HEART OF THE GAME: TICKET PRICE**
• Higher fares mean fewer passengers; cheap tickets fill the bus but leave money on the table
• A live profit curve with an "ideal zone" helps you find the sweet spot
• The ideal price shifts as your fleet grows — good managers keep adjusting
• Fair prices and comfortable buses raise your reputation, and reputation raises demand everywhere

**FROM MINIBUS TO VIP**
• 6 segments, 10 models: minibus, midibus, intercity, comfort, VIP 2+1 and double-decker
• Every vehicle has a role: minibuses shine on short routes and lose riders on long ones
• Buy new or used, trade in, overhaul or sell
• Manage breakdowns, wear, fuel, drivers, tolls and terminal fees

**GROW YOUR STATIONS**
• Your own bus terminal in every city: step inside and watch passengers and buses
• Comfy seats, a tea-and-simit kiosk, a digital departures board and more: 16 upgrades
• Upgrades raise the station's popularity and the passengers on routes departing from it

**EASY TO MANAGE**
• Tap two cities, check the quote card, open the route — three taps
• The planner suggests the most profitable destinations
• One-glance route status: "Not enough capacity", "Low occupancy", "Losing money"
• The maintenance assistant overhauls worn buses and repairs broken ones, sending them back to their routes
• An 18-step goal chain teaches the game step by step

**CLEAN AND SMOOTH**
• A clean, readable map with colourful routes and moving buses
• Day-night cycle
• An interface designed for phones and tablets

**GOAL: $10,000,000**
Reach ten million dollars in cash and become the Bus Emperor! Careful: stay in the red for 30 days and you go bankrupt.

• Fully offline, no internet needed
• No ads, no in-app purchases
• Automatic saving
• Interface language: Turkish
